## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Each first-impression screen says what, why and what next
The welcome screen, the desktop and the opening of each of the four steps SHALL tell the student, in plain short sentences, what is happening, why it helps and what to do next. The tutor stays Spanish-only in audio; English appears only as subtitles, and the tone stays cheeky, encouraging and school-safe, naming at most one thing to fix.

#### Scenario: Opening a step
- **WHEN** a student opens any of the four steps for the first time
- **THEN** the screen shows one line each for what this step is, why it helps and the single next action

#### Scenario: No dead ends
- **WHEN** a step is locked or empty
- **THEN** the screen says why and what unlocks it
