The idea

A Windows 98 desktop pasted over a lost world: a child's painted sun with eyes cut from a toy photo, a playground that never ends, a pink office full of CRTs that watch you. Everything is oversaturated, low-res and shot on a camcorder. Bicho Guay lives in it: a paper flower with one giant eye for a head, and no mouth.

Rules for the weird

Bicho never dies or loses anything, and never guilt-trips.

Uncanny, never scary: no jump scares, no gore, no sudden sound.
