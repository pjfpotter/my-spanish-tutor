Tele

An old television with one enormous eye, broadcasting Castilian Spanish from somewhere between an empty mall and a field under a rainbow. Tele has no mouth: feelings live in the eye, the antennae, the picture and the world around it.

Where this could go next

clips

A bank of baked clips

Each scripted line ships as audio plus a tiny timing file: word times (for the caption), loudness (for the glow) and a feeling track. No lip-sync means no alignment tools; ElevenLabs word timestamps are enough.

pronunciation

Mouths move to a diagram

Without a mouth, sounds like the Castilian /θ/ or the rolled rr get their own side-on mouth diagram, shown only in pronunciation moments.

art

Almost any tool works

The rig is an eye, two antennae and a screen effect. That fits Lottie, Spine, Live2D or layered images, or even short eye loops filmed or rendered and played on the screen.

worlds

One liminal room per lesson

The background already reacts to feelings. Each unit could live in its own place: the endless mall, the pool rooms, the field with the single door.
