      Try this

      1. Free time. The card now shows your real sentence. Tap “+ Add more” and pick “sin embargo”: only it stays open in the connector list, and “Odio” opens up after it. “Pero” is refused until there is something to contrast.
      2. Technology. “On what?” leaves only the two device phrases open.
      3. What to wear. Add up to four things: “a red jumper, blue jeans y white trainers”.
      4. Bedroom. Each sentence is its own frame. Half-built ones end in “…” and fail the check.
      5. Check a sentence: it now blocks only what is ungrammatical or impossible, and credits anything that could be true.
