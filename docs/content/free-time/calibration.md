# Free time: answer calibration

Question: **¿Qué haces en tu tiempo libre?** (What do you do in your free time?)

Calibrated by the teacher (the developer) for a Year 8 class in an average UK comprehensive. Use this as the reference for what each level looks like when drafting, validating or scoring answers for this course. Sentence builder: [sentence-builder.md](sentence-builder.md).

## Level names

The teacher's file used friendlier labels. The app and specs use survival, good and showing off.

| App and specs | Teacher's file |
|---|---|
| survival | Good Enough |
| good | Really Good |
| showing off | Amazing |

## Teacher's rules for each level

- **Survival:** one verb phrase and one justified opinion. One sentence is enough. Many students have literacy difficulties in English, and memorising "Me gusta jugar al fútbol porque es divertido" is already a real challenge in Year 8. Do not ask for three activities.
- **Good:** two activities at most, plus one extra feature such as a contrast (*sin embargo*, *aunque*), *suelo* with a frequency, or *lo hago con…*. More than that is too much to memorise for most 13 year olds in their second year of Spanish. A good grade is reachable with less.
- **Showing off:** long answers are right at this level. Only a few students will push as far as they can. Amazing answers carry the risk of trying to memorise too much in too little time.

## Teacher's notes

- In a 5 question speaking test at KS3 level, one or two answers at the showing off level are enough to score top marks.
- Verb + infinitive is scored highly in the spec and is always a stumbling block for students.
- When this sentence builder was used with a real class, students avoided *lo hago* and *lo hacemos* "like the plague".
- Survival answers would get a Grade 4 pass at GCSE if they were consistently accurate and included 3 verb tenses and two justified opinions across the whole picture task or general conversation section.

## Teacher's original model answers (verbatim)

These are the calibration source. They use a few words that are not in the sentence builder, such as "pero", "y", "mi hermana", "mis amigas" and "Sábado, voy a bailar". Treat them as a guide to length and features, not as a word list.

**Good Enough (survival)**

> Me gusta jugar al fútbol porque es divertido. Me encanta jugar a los videojuegos porque es emocionante. Me gusta ver la tele porque es relajante.

**Really Good (good)**

> Me gusta jugar al baloncesto porque es bueno para la salud pero no me gusta leer libros porque es aburrido. Suelo chatear con mis amigos todos los días porque me hace reir. Sin embargo, no me gusta ir al cine.  Los fines de semana prefiero escuchar música, lo hago con mi hermana aunque a veces es aburrido.

**Amazing (showing off)**

> Tengo mucho tiempo libre. ¡Qué guay! Suelo montar en bici por la tarde y a veces me gusta leer libros ya que me relaja. Me gustaría tocar la guitarra. Sábado, voy a bailar con mis amigas.  No tengo mucho tiempo libre por los deberes. ¡Qué rollo! No me gusta hacer deporte aunque es bueno para la salud. Me gustaría salir con mis amigos porque me hace reir. Lo hacemos los sábados. Suelo jugar al fútbol aunque me gustaría jugar al baloncesto. Odio jugar a los videojuegos porque es aburrido. Me gusta ver la tele, lo hago con mi familia de vez en cuando.

The teacher's first survival model has three sentences. The rule above (one sentence is enough) is the later, firmer calibration. The generated answers below follow the rules, not the length of these originals.

## Notes for validation

- Every adjective in the reasons is masculine because the subject is an infinitive. Answers that use a feminine form should be flagged.
- *Lo hago* and *lo hacemos* are the features students avoid most. Count them as a clear sign of the showing off level, and expect to scaffold them.
