# Promo video storyboard (draft, 16 Nov 2026)

Task 30.14 in `openspec/changes/ks3-spanish-speaking-tutor-v1/tasks.md`. Look and assets: `animation-plan.md`.

**Format (decided 16 Nov):** 9:16 vertical, about 45 seconds. No spoken narration: captions, TELE's real voice, app sounds and music only. English appears only as subtitles, never in TELE's voice.

**Story:** a 13-year-old with a speaking test next week. Revising is boring, speaking aloud feels weird, she never knows if she said it right, and she has lost her notes. She decides not to think about it. A nightmare: a monster teacher chases her down a corridor asking questions she cannot understand. The dream turns into dreamcore paradise, TELE arrives, and we show the app. Last beat: she smashes the test.

**Look:** the worry world is blue biro on lined paper. Colour bleeds in when the app arrives, and the app is full-colour Win98 dreamcore.

| # | Time | What we see | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-4s | Dolores at a desk, drawing instead of revising. "Speaking test: next week" is on the fridge calendar. | Lo-fi, slightly flat |
| 2 | 4-11s | Fast beats: flashcards are dull, her head drops. She mutters Spanish to the bedroom wall, feels odd, stops mid-word. "Was that right?" with no way to know. Notes lost, the bag is chaos. | Quick stabs, a tape stop |

## Footage to record (task 30.14a)

## Guardrails

- The teacher is a monster in the dream and a normal person on test day, so it cannot read as a real teacher.
- Dolores is an original drawn character. No real student, no student data, no copyrighted characters.
- TELE's audio is the rebuilt `eleven_v4` voice, never edge-tts.
- Check `docs/design-direction.md` before storyboarding TELE close-ups: `animation-plan.md` says TELE has one eye and no mouth, and its screen glows with the voice loudness.
