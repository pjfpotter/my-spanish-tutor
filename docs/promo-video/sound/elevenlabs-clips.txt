MY SPANISH TUTOR - PROMO VIDEO "BLANK"
ElevenLabs voice clips

SETUP
- Model: Eleven v3 (it is the model that understands [audio tags]).
- Stability: Creative or Natural, so the tags have room to act.
- Generate each clip as its own file, named by its ID (V1.mp3, V2.mp3, ...).
- Make 3 to 4 takes of each and pick the best. V5 matters most.
- Commercial use needs a paid plan. Do not use the free tier.
- TELE: use one voice for every TELE clip. If it will become the app voice,
  choose it as the production voice (edge-tts is prototype-only).
- Teacher and Dolores need a Spanish-capable voice. Use different voices.
- TELE says "we", never "I". Dolores is they/them.
- Do not change a clip's words after the mouth animation is made from it.

[playful] Hear it. [short pause] Say it. [short pause] Again.

AUDIO TAG CHEAT SHEET (if a tag does not work, delete it and re-take)
[short pause]  [long pause]  [sighs]  [whispers]  [excited]  [curious]
[sarcastic]  [laughs]  [deadpan]  [wry]  [warm]  [playful]
Tags are suggestions to the model, not commands. Re-roll takes until
the delivery matches the note above each clip.
Do not stack more than 2 tags in a row.

DURATION CHECK
Target about 30 s total. TELE clips V2-V5 plus V7 should come to about
18 to 20 seconds with pauses. If it runs over, drop the V4 "Nobody to
check the answers" line first, then the third take of V5.
